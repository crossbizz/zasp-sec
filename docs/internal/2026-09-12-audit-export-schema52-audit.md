## Rollback distinction

52Down must refuse retained export requests/snapshots/artifact bindings/jobs/grants/receipts or other export evidence defined by the approved contract, including unfinished or unknown-outcome work. Do not delete those records to make rollback pass. If append-only audit records are part of new export evidence, include that retention case explicitly.

Existing runtime51 evidence is **not** a reason to refuse52→51: precision V2 batches, frozen candidate snapshots, Strong/unknown session events, receipts and search checkpoints remain representable on51. Seed real existing runtime work/evidence, install52, perform no export, then52Down should preserve all those bytes and restore the exact51 functions/grants/fingerprint. A subsequent51→50 attempt must still encounter its original retained-precision-evidence refusal. Keep those two rollback decisions separate.
